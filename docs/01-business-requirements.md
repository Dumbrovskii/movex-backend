# Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for the MoveX platform. It describes the business objectives, stakeholders, capabilities, constraints, and success criteria that guide product development.

This document intentionally avoids implementation details. Technical design, architecture, and system behavior are described in separate documents.

---
# Business Objectives

The MoveX platform aims to achieve the following business objectives:

- Enable passengers to request transportation quickly and conveniently.
- Connect passengers with suitable drivers in real time.
- Maximize driver utilization.
- Reduce passenger waiting time.
- Provide transparent trip pricing.
- Ensure a safe and reliable transportation experience.
- Support sustainable business growth across multiple geographic regions.

# Business Constraints

The platform operates under the following constraints:

- Transportation services are limited to supported geographic regions.
- Drivers must satisfy platform eligibility requirements.
- Only verified drivers may accept ride requests.
- Every completed trip must be associated with exactly one passenger and one driver.
- Payment is required before a trip can be considered successfully completed.
- Trips must be traceable for operational and customer support purposes.

---
# Business Assumptions

The following assumptions are considered valid for the initial product version:

- Drivers own or legally operate their vehicles.
- Passengers possess internet-connected mobile devices.
- Drivers have continuous internet connectivity while accepting rides.
- GPS location data is available during active trips.
- Electronic payment methods are available to users.

---
# Business Risks

Potential risks include:

- Insufficient driver availability.
- Uneven passenger demand across regions.
- Payment failures.
- Fraudulent user activity.
- Regulatory changes affecting ride-hailing services.

# Business Success Criteria

The MoveX platform will be considered successful when it:

- Enables passengers to consistently obtain transportation.
- Enables drivers to efficiently receive ride requests.
- Delivers a reliable and predictable transportation experience.
- Supports sustainable platform growth.
- Builds trust among passengers and drivers.
